# Olympus: The Flagship

**Olympus** is the entry point to the Draconis ecosystem — a performance system for athletes, coaches and engineers who measure everything.

It sits at the top of the stack for a reason: if you can’t measure it, you can’t improve it.

## Principles

- **Measured** — no vibes, only signal.
- **Open** — [github.com/draconis-engineering](https://github.com/draconis-engineering).
- **Fast** — heavy math, lean runtime.
